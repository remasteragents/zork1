# Modern shortcuts: X for EXAMINE, Z for WAIT, G for AGAIN.
# Each shortcut is checked in play next to the original verb it stands for.

? ZORK I
?room West of House

# The original verbs still work.
examine mailbox
? mailbox is closed
look at mailbox
? mailbox is closed
describe mailbox
? mailbox is closed
wait
? time passes

# X is EXAMINE.
x mailbox
? mailbox is closed
?! don't know the word
x house
? house
?! don't know the word

# G repeats the last command, just like AGAIN.
x mailbox
? mailbox is closed
g
? mailbox is closed
again
? mailbox is closed

# Z is WAIT, and G repeats it.
z
? time passes
g
? time passes
?room West of House

# X works on things you carry too.
open mailbox
take leaflet
? taken
x leaflet
? welcome to zork
